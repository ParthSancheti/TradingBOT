Installation guide

A connected desk, in three steps.

Once your administrator access is enrolled, setup connects your own Google Sheet, sources and Chrome browser. This guide contains no private account data.

1. Your Google Sheet is the business database.

Sign in to the desk, open Setup & daily settings and download the Apps Script files. Add the signing secret to Script properties, run the workbook setup, and deploy the endpoint. Paste its URL and secret into the protected setup form.

Your inventory, requests and tasks stay in Google Sheets. Credentials stay in the OS credential store.

2. Choose the people and rules.

Add each inventory sender with an international phone number and a recognizable name. Add buyer numbers separately. Set the cost currency, margin, business timezone, quiet hours and model ID. Daily changes use the same settings screen.

Phone prefixes provide country hints. Confirm a contact's timezone before scheduling their replies.

3. Start Chrome and WhatsApp.

The desktop helper is installed once by the operator. Add the Chrome extension and register its exact ID. In the desk, choose Start desktop & open WhatsApp. Sign in to WhatsApp, then start observation in the extension popup.

Each minute starts a bounded scan of configured sources. Keep the computer awake. Actual scan age is reported; chats not observed are never called up to date.

Before first use

The operator must configure independently verified administrator sign-in and trusted HTTPS access. A forwarded dashboard link grants no access. The observation build does not send customer messages or commit trades.

Google consent, model/data-provider accounts and WhatsApp login must be completed by their account owner. There are no shared built-in credentials.
